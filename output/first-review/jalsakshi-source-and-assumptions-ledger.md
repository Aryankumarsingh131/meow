## C. Computed results

**Conservative**: Year 1 revenue ₹1.0 L (subscription ₹0.4 L, setup ₹0.6 L); Year 1 operating result −₹8.0 L with founders unpaid, −₹23.0 L with stipends; ARR at end of Year 1 ₹1.8 L. Year 3 revenue ₹16.6 L, gross margin 38%, operating result −₹34.8 L, ARR at end of Year 3 ₹19.1 L, break-even ≈ 42 customers. Impact (Year-3 deployment, per year): 6,360 screening records, 318 flagged, 57 followed up at the 18% external benchmark vs 159 at the 50% target.

**Base**: Year 1 revenue ₹2.9 L (subscription ₹1.4 L, setup ₹1.5 L); Year 1 operating result −₹6.6 L with founders unpaid, −₹21.6 L with stipends; ARR at end of Year 1 ₹4.9 L. Year 3 revenue ₹44.7 L, gross margin 53%, operating result −₹35.5 L, ARR at end of Year 3 ₹50.6 L, break-even ≈ 58 customers. Impact (Year-3 deployment, per year): 16,860 screening records, 843 flagged, 152 followed up at the 18% external benchmark vs 632 at the 75% target.

**Optimistic**: Year 1 revenue ₹6.0 L (subscription ₹3.1 L, setup ₹2.9 L); Year 1 operating result −₹4.6 L with founders unpaid, −₹19.6 L with stipends; ARR at end of Year 1 ₹10.3 L. Year 3 revenue ₹93.3 L, gross margin 60%, operating result −₹25.3 L, ARR at end of Year 3 ₹106.2 L, break-even ≈ 77 customers. Impact (Year-3 deployment, per year): 35,400 screening records, 1,770 flagged, 319 followed up at the 18% external benchmark vs 1,593 at the 90% target.
