## Workflow walkthrough (illustrative shape only)

A single fictional walkthrough of the water-screening-to-followup job, structured
so a real interview could later replace each row without changing the shape.

| Step | What (fictional) | Observation vs. inference |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worker fictionally visits a water source and performs a kit test | **Observation** (fictional: claimed to have been watched) |
| 2 | Worker fictionally records the result on paper, then later re-enters it into a spreadsheet | **Observation** (fictional: claimed to have been watched) |
| 3 | Delay between paper record and spreadsheet entry is fictionally "usually a day or two" | **Inference** (fictional: this is the interviewer's summary of what the worker said, not a timed measurement) |
| 4 | Supervisor fictionally reviews spreadsheet weekly and calls out overdue follow-ups | **Observation** (fictional: claimed to have been watched) |
| 5 | Worker fictionally "would prefer a phone app over paper" | **Inference** (fictional: stated preference, not observed behavior) |

The Observation/Inference column exists specifically so a real interviewer
cannot collapse "I watched this happen" and "the worker told me this is what
usually happens" into one undifferentiated note. Every row above is fictional
regardless of which column it sits in — the *distinction itself* is the part
of the template being demonstrated.
